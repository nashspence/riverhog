# Finite closure and real-consumer challenges

The current #968 issue controls. This matrix is a route for its acceptance, not
an extra contract inventory. Synthetic examples illustrate only part of it;
integration must exercise the actual collectors, adapters and consumers.

## Completion boundary

Accounting is complete when the entire maintained check universe has reviewed
purpose and relationship disposition, all relevant producers/consumers preserve
those meanings, and the required source-bound regressions and normal gates pass.
Every missing product proof remains honestly visible under its existing owner.
"Unestablished product behavior" does not itself mean "unfinished accounting".
Unclassified checks, silent unmatched bindings and misleading consumers do.

Do not require all product obligations to have passing proof to finish #968;
#873/#964/#492 and final #469 qualification retain that work and their gates.
An unresolved intended-product relationship must be bound/corrected or given an
explicit reviewed owner/reason/finding; an "unknown" blanket exemption is not
closure. Requiredness is never relaxed by classification. A truly obsolete test
expectation is corrected only after resolving current commitments and supported
versions, not simply because the new inventory lacks its old target.

## A01 — discovery, purpose and ownership (P01)

Reconcile actual pytest nodes, class/parameter cases and maintained non-pytest work.
Show product, implementation, repository-policy and validation-tooling checks,
including mixed groups and a supplied component's own public contract.
Add/remove/rename a node, parameter, job and unsupported declaration; require an
explicit result rather than stale links or an inferred non-product default.
Keep execution grouping and requiredness independent. A generator testing its own
inventory is insufficient completeness evidence: compare to actual collection/jobs.

## A02 — expectation authority and both directions (P01, P03)

Identify the owning expectation and exactly what the check establishes. Challenge
self-derived expectations and two implementations agreeing on the same wrong value.
Also prove legitimate agreement, structural and rejection promises: none is excluded
merely for not being a positive runtime lifecycle. Shared normative helpers are
allowed; automatic restatement of the implementation under test is not an oracle.
Expose no-witness obligations, unbound product-intent tests, stale and retired
expectations separately. Counts of tests, groups, links and obligations stay distinct.

## A03 — observation, assertion and sufficient proof (P00, P02)

| Case | Expected meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Cursor fixture succeeds in all selected applications | Exact local SQLite/ASGI restart support; broader release claims remain unestablished. |
| Policy and tooling tests also pass | Required results are retained; neither adds cursor proof. |
| A node contains product and policy checks | Attribute only the relevant successful group, without forcing one purpose for the file. |
| One necessary check or parameter is missing | Some execution may pass; the complete required claim does not. |
| A negative behavior is correctly rejected | The rejection promise may be proved; a positive lifecycle is not inferred. |
| Symbols/schema agree but runtime is unexercised | Candidate/structural evidence only, at its actual scope. |
| Missing oracle, unrelated oracle, or self-derived expectation | No promotion to stronger behavior. |
| One relevant assertion is removed/bypassed or its result corrupted | Product support disappears or the focused regression fails; unrelated green checks do not rescue it. |

Attribution metadata does not prove its own truth. Use reviewed source and a small
set of actual wrong-output/check-sensitivity regressions, not a declaration that
all possible future assertion deletion is mechanically detectable.

## A04 — result integrity, scopes and selected executions (P02)

Exercise wrong source/work/conditions/parameter, stale or duplicate artifacts,
missing required group, failed setup/call/teardown, skip, xfail, xpass, interrupted
and incomplete runs. Reject invalid evidence; preserve valid failed outcomes.
Do not replace a required error with a generic product-failure assertion or vice versa.

Exercise controller/worker duplication, old and selected attempts, and the actual
supported exact-job-origin reuse across a partial Actions rerun. A new blanket
ban on reuse would regress the current foundation. An unrelated retained failed
fixture must not poison a successful owner; a required failed descendant must
not disappear. Keep the public request's expected error separate from the enclosing
check's successful assertion of that error.

A broad route name is not proof that each consumer interprets the state correctly.

unestablished broader claim present together. Accounting-only changes cannot alter
normative Closure meaning or identity. Preserve source trace and limitations at
normal entry points, not only hidden JSON. No redesign of the accepted renderer.

## A06 — independent gates and advisory performance (P02–P04)

Required product, policy, implementation, tooling and evidence-integrity failures
remain blocking under their existing rules. A complete truthful failed-run report
is not a successful run. Goals absent/changed/removed, severe timing misses and
unavailable comparisons do not change required-work identity, contracts, eligibility,
exit status or approval-to-proceed requirements. Independent safety/resource and
growth obligations continue to own their failures. No missing-test waiver through
a performance label; no performance gate through an evidence label.

## A08 — final reconciliation and stopping rule (P04)

In #968, map A01–A07 to actual final-source tests/results and real consumer output.
Record full maintained-universe counts and dispositions, current mandatory gate
coverage, code/contract identities, residual owning proof findings, and required
local and independent exact-source GitHub evidence. No universal proof assertion,
all-green badge, synthetic suite or first-slice result substitutes for this review.
Stop when the finite boundary holds. Broader flaky-test/performance work, mass
renames, public taxonomy redesign and repository-wide mutation testing are not
additional gates. Unexpected concrete findings get their normal owning disposition.
